## SUMMARY
ADVERSARIAL VERDICT: the thesis FAILS in its strong form and survives only heavily qualified. Methodological note first: this subtask inherited a session whose 200-call WebSearch budget was already exhausted (my queries were refused) and whose proxy policy-blocks FRED/Treasury/stooq egress (403), so no new independent searches were possible; every number below traces to the dated, sourced observations harvested by this session's earlier live searches (CNBC, Bloomberg, Reuters, Goldman-via-Bloomberg, CME FedWatch-style odds, NY Fed ACM/FRED THREEFYTP10, Treasury MTS, Polymarket) — none from pre-window memory. (1) "Front end stayed relatively anchored" is false. The 2Y rose from 4.07% (Jun 26) to 4.298% (Aug 28), +23bp net, with a violent round trip: Sep-2026 hike odds went ~55-63% (late June) → ~82% with Dec at 92% (~two hikes priced, Jul 23 oil spike) → 17% July-hike odds after the -0.4% m/m June CPI (Jul 14) → 44% after -23k payrolls (Aug 7) → 42% after 3.4% July CPI (Aug 12) → 46% after Warsh's hawkish Jackson Hole debut (Aug 28, 2Y +6.6bp that day, dollar +0.4%). The June 17 SEP was itself a hawkish policy shock (median end-2026 dot up to 3.8%, erasing a cut; 9 of 19 officials penciling hikes), and the Jul 29 FOMC drew three hike dissents. Goldman's first fully-priced hike slipped only from Dec 2026 to Jan 2027; a Sep cut was priced ~1% (Aug 18). That is a material hawkish repricing of Fed policy, not anchoring. (2) The "bear steepening" claim holds only intra-window. On endpoints, 10s2s was ~37bp (4.44 vs 4.07) in late June and ~38bp (4.68 vs 4.298) on Aug 27-28 — zero net steepening; 30s2s went ~84bp → ~90bp, only +6bp net. The genuine steepening (30s2s ~115bp) occurred in the Aug 7-18 stretch when soft data rallied the front end to ~4.14% while 30s ran to 5.31-5.33% — and it unwound after the Aug 19 buyback doubling and the Aug 28 Warsh repricing. (3) Quantification: the +23bp 2Y move, passed through even 1-for-1, explains ~80-95% of the 10Y's ~25-28bp net rise and ~80% of the 30Y's net +29bp (55% of its +42bp peak); at a conservative 0.5-0.7 beta it still explains roughly half the 10Y move. The measured ACM term-premium rise (+30-35bp: 0.513% June avg → 0.87% July, 0.80% Aug 13) is real and roughly co-equal, corroborated by tailing 20Y/30Y auctions and Treasury's emergency buyback expansion — but it cannot be "most" of the story when the front end moved as much as the belly. (4) Data-quality flag: the term-premium workstream's Ferrante-based claim ("two cuts priced; Sep cut odds 72%→94% after an Aug 21 Jackson Hole") contradicts the verified 2026 record (cut odds ~1%, hike ~4x likelier, Jackson Hole Aug 28, hawkish reaction) and matches the Aug-2025 Powell pattern — almost certainly a stale-2025 artifact; discard it. HONEST CONCLUSION: summer 2026 was a joint repricing — a hawkish Fed-path shock (SEP, oil, dissents, Warsh) plus a genuine fiscal/term-premium premium concentrated at 20-30Y. The thesis needs rewriting: keep the term-premium component as a co-driver of the long end's extra ~10-20bp and the mid-August steepening episode; drop "front end anchored" and "NOT a Fed repricing" entirely.
