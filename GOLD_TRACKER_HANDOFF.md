## 2. Current regime snapshot (as of Jul 29, 2026)

- Gold peaked above **$5,500/oz intraday in January**, broke below **$4,000 in late June**; roughly **–7% YTD** but still a top performer over 12 months.
- WGC base case: consolidation, **~$4,100 ±5% through year-end**, consistent with consensus of at least one **Fed hike by October**, parallel tightening from BoJ/ECB/BoE, and US inflation peaking near **3.9% in Q2**.
- Upside break above **~$4,500** likely requires a clear global growth deceleration signal or a geopolitical/rates shock; structural central-bank and long-term investor demand seen limiting downside.
- H1 lesson: gold is trading **geopolitics (US–Iran) and positioning momentum** more than its textbook real-rate beta, and **Asian markets are gaining weight in price discovery**.

**Working regime label:** *Consolidation after blow-off; positioning unwound; macro anchor = Fed hiking path vs. geopolitical risk premium.* Re-label this each note — regime first, data second.

## 3. Tracker dashboard

Each tracker: what it is, why it matters in THIS regime, source, cadence, and the level that matters.

### T1. Price, momentum & vol
- XAU spot, weekly change, distance from 50/200dma; GVZ (gold vol) level and skew (risk-reversal) for direction of hedging demand.
- **Levels:** $3,900 (June low / WGC band floor), $4,100 (base-case anchor), $4,500 (breakout trigger).
- Source: Bloomberg/TradingView. Cadence: continuous; snapshot in note.

### T2. Real rates & central bank path
- US 10y TIPS real yield; 2y nominal; market-implied probability of the October Fed hike; BoJ/ECB/BoE path.
- Why: the base case is *rangebound because of* the hiking path — if the October hike gets priced out, the range assumption breaks.
- Source: FRED, CME FedWatch. Cadence: weekly.

### T3. USD & Asian FX (the Clark lead)
- DXY, plus **CNY, JPY, KRW, INR** vs USD. Clark's argument: gold does well when **Asian currencies are strong**; Asian FX has led gold better than US real rates in this cycle.
- Track: ADXY or simple 4-ccy basket, 1m change vs gold 1m change; note divergences explicitly.
- Source: Bloomberg/FRED. Cadence: weekly.

### T4. Central bank buying
- Monthly official-sector purchases (WGC/IMF IFS), with the China (PBoC) series called out separately; note reporting lags and unreported buying estimates.
- Why: the structural bid under the market; Clark's "diversification out of Treasuries post-Russia-freeze" thesis lives or dies here. Also track **custody proxies** (Fed custody holdings of Treasuries for foreign officials) as the other side of the trade.
- Source: WGC Goldhub monthly stats, IMF IFS. Cadence: monthly.

### T5. ETF flows
- Weekly global gold ETF flows, split **North America / Europe / Asia**. Asian ETF flows are the marginal price-setter this cycle — flag any week where Asia and the West diverge.
- Source: WGC Goldhub flow data. Cadence: weekly.

### T6. Futures positioning
- CFTC COT: managed money net length (contracts and %ile vs 5y); open interest change on rallies vs selloffs.
- Why: H1 taught us momentum/profit-taking dominates short-term; positioning tells you how crowded the next move is.
- Source: CFTC (Friday release). Cadence: weekly.

### T7. Asian physical demand
- Shanghai (SGE) premium/discount to London; India domestic premium; seasonality (festival/wedding demand into Q4).
- Why: physical premiums confirm or deny whether Asia is absorbing Western selling.
- Source: SGE, dealer sheets, WGC. Cadence: weekly.

### T8. Geopolitical risk premium
- US–Iran situation status; Geopolitical Risk (GPR) index; oil as the cross-check (if gold is up on geopolitics, oil usually agrees).
- Estimate the residual: gold move unexplained by rates+USD ≈ current risk premium. Flag when the premium is large (fragile) vs small (room to build).
- Cadence: per note.
