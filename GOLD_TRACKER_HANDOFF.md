## 1. The assignment, restated

Track gold and deliver **meaningful insight every two weeks** — not a price recap. Adam can see the price. The note earns its existence only if it answers:

1. **What changed** in the drivers (not the price) since the last note?
2. **What does the market believe**, and where is that belief crowded or fragile?
3. **What would change my mind** — falsifiable trigger levels?
4. **So what** — is there an actionable asymmetry (direction, structure, or RV)?

## 5. Tracker dashboard (ordered by GRAM weight)

### T1. Price, vol & technicals
- Spot, weekly Δ, 30d realised vs 1m ATM implied vol, distance from 2y avg (~$3,520).
- **Levels:** $3,860 technical support (WGC: break opens another leg down) · $4,100 base-case anchor · $4,500 uptrend trigger · $5,000 strong-signal target.
- **Drawdown context:** current ~25% off peak. Historical >20% drawdowns (8 since 1971): median 29%, average 36%. Median stabilisation from the $5,405 peak ≈ **$3,840 — confluent with the $3,860 technical**. That zone is the highest-information area on the board.
- Cadence: continuous; snapshot per note.

### T2. Momentum & positioning (top GRAM driver — 24%)
- CFTC COT managed-money net length (contracts + 5y percentile); open interest Δ on rallies vs selloffs; gold options activity/skew (options froth flagged at the January top); ETF flow momentum (T7).
- Question each note: *is the next 10% move the crowded one or the pain trade?*
- Source: CFTC (Fri), Bloomberg. Cadence: weekly.

### T3. Asian FX & China retail flows (the Clark lead)
- **FX:** CNY, JPY, KRW, INR vs USD (or ADXY); DXY as the aggregate. Clark: gold does well when Asian currencies are strong.
- **China retail flow proxies** (per Clark's *Tracking China Retail Gold Flows* — refine once full note is in hand): SGE withdrawals (monthly), SGE premium/discount to London (daily), China gold ETF creations, jewellery/bar & coin trend, **insurance pilot allocations** (top insurers permitted into gold since 2025 — a structural sticky bid).
- **Session split:** cumulative gold return in Asian vs US trading hours (WGC method). If Asia stops buying the dip, the floor thesis is broken — this is the single cleanest tell.
- Cadence: weekly; SGE withdrawals monthly.

### T4. Risk & geopolitical premium (17%)
- US–Iran status (watch India oil-supply spillover → T8); GPR index level and monthly Δ (**+100pts ≈ +2.5%** on gold); private credit stress (BoE flagged; flight-to-quality catalyst); equity vol and AI-valuation scrutiny.
- Estimate the residual risk premium: gold move unexplained by rates+FX. Large premium = fragile; small = room to build.
- Cadence: per note.

### T5. Rates, Fed & the October hike
- Fed funds futures odds of the **October hike** — the load-bearing assumption of the ±5% range. Also: 10y and TIPS (–25bp ≈ +1.75%), dot-plot skew (median hawkish but half of entries at/below current — genuinely split, and **Warsh declined to join the dot plot**), Trump administration pressure for cuts, **Fed-independence headlines** (shook markets in January; hits both yields *and* the dollar's reserve role — the most gold-bullish tail on the board).
- Nuance from WGC: a delivered hike is not automatically bearish — what matters is whether tightening *raises Fed credibility* (bearish gold) or reads as politically contested (bullish).
- Cadence: weekly.

### T6. Central banks (outside GRAM but structural)
- Monthly official purchases (WGC/IMF IFS; note lags + unreported buying). Baseline ~600t/yr long-term avg; ~1,000t/yr pace since 2022; **±20–30t vs baseline ≈ ±1%**.
- Q1 2026 saw **tactical selling/swaps** — watch whether Q2/Q3 confirms deceleration (headwind) or reversion. CB Gold Reserves Survey says intent remains, but intent ≠ tonnage.
- Signal channel: gold performs strongly when CB + investment share of total demand >30% — track that share quarterly.
- Cross-check: foreign official Treasury custody at the Fed (FT reported foreign CBs selling USTs after the Iran war — the diversification trade's other leg).
- Cadence: monthly.

### T7. ETF & sticky institutional flows
- Weekly global ETF flows split **NA / Europe / Asia** — flag every week Asia and the West diverge (H1 pattern: US sells, Asia absorbs).
- Long-term owners: SWFs, pensions, endowments, China insurers — the WGC's "sticky flows" that cap the downside. Slower-moving; review monthly.
- Cadence: weekly.

### T8. India — policy wildcard
- Import duty **6% → 15%** (April), plus demand-discouraging messaging, to defend FX reserves under INR pressure. WGC econometrics: duty alone cuts demand **50–60t (~10% y/y)**; believed priced in — a **rollback is the unpriced bullish surprise**.
- Watch: INR, India domestic premium/discount (discount = duty biting), oil (the cause), festival/wedding season into Q4 (income effect test), **gold-loan defaults** (collateral liquidation = supply).
- Cadence: biweekly; deeper quarterly.

### T9. US midterms (November 2026)
- Ratings: Dems favoured for the House, GOP the Senate → split-Congress policy uncertainty is gold-supportive; US bar & coin demand historically rises under Democratic control.
- Low weight until October; escalate as the election approaches.
- Cadence: monthly, then biweekly from Oct.
